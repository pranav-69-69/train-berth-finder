Kaise use karein?

Berth Finder kaise kaam karta hai?

Apne ticket wala coach chuno (Sleeper, AC 3 Tier, AC 2 Tier, Chair Car…), phir berth / seat number likho. Turant pata chalega — Lower, Middle, Upper ya Side — aur window side hai ya aisle side. Neeche poora coach 3D view mein dikhega, aapki berth peeli highlighted.

Kaunse coach covered hain?

Sleeper (1–80), AC 3 Tier (1–72), AC 2 Tier (1–54), AC 3 Tier Economy / Garib Rath (1–83), AC Chair Car (1–78), Executive Chair Car (1–56), Second Sitting (1–108). First AC (1A) covered nahi hai — cabins aur coupe ka koi fixed pattern nahi hota.
